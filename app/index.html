Central Texas Water Ledger

What the official gauges said, when, and how that compares with each gauge's own record. A reading describes a station; nothing here is a forecast or a verdict about a place.

Pick a marker

Stations show the latest reading with its time and freshness, the six-hour trend, the 48-hour peak, the same-time-of-year percentile, and the station's own event thresholds. Wells and lakes show level and 7- and 30-day change. Freshness is judged against the bundle's own timestamp.

Sources: USGS, Texas Water Development Board (Water Data for Texas), Edwards Aquifer Authority, National Weather Service. Map tiles and creek lines © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Repository: saulbanov/hill-country-hydro.
