READING THIS MAP

Channels connect.
Measurements stay local.

Below both Llano gauges the mapped channel reaches Lake Lyndon B. Johnson first. Below both Pedernales gauges it reaches Lake Travis first. The Highland Lakes are a chain of managed reservoirs on the Colorado River.

Continuous lines show the mapped channels. The short colored stroke and the dot at a gauge describe that gauge’s reading. No reach-wide estimate is supported here.

Austin’s six gauged creeks are a named group on the same map, with the City of Austin rain gauges. They are not a watershed outline.

Well locations are grouped by surface watershed. That does not establish which river a well supplies. Reservoir storage also changes with releases and withdrawals; a rise does not measure rainfall-driven inflow.

Methods and coverage

Daily flow ranks compare each gauge with its own Approved daily means in the same ±15 calendar days during 2006–2025. At least five qualifying years and 80% coverage are required. Instantaneous storm readings receive no daily percentile.

Rain symbols show individual rolling 24-hour reports, never basin averages. No overlapping totals are added. Groundwater depth is feet below land surface: a larger depth means a lower level.
