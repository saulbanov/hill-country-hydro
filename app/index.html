WHAT “USUAL” MEANS

A daily reference,
from the same gauge

The median of daily flow for September 16–October 16 in 2006–2025. The storm readings are instantaneous. This contrast shows flow above a usual day; it does not rank how rare an instantaneous peak was.

Each stroke sits at a named gauge crossing. The rest of the creek stays dashed because a point measurement does not establish flow along the whole channel.

Methods, coverage & limits

One primary USGS sensor per gauge. Repeated captures retain conflicting values; the latest retrieval is shown and flagged. Snapshots use prior readings at most 30 minutes old. Gaps are not interpolated.

Widths use five bands and stop increasing above 500 cfs of excess flow. Exact values remain in the list. A cfs is one cubic foot per second. Colors describe a measurement comparison, not flood severity or safety.
