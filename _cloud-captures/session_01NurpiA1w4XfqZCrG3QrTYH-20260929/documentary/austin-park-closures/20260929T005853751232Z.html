Closed Parks and Buildings

Austin Parks and Recreation posts all closures on this page. To notify us about any closures or park or facility issues, please submit a request through Austin 3-1-1.  

Starting November 1, 2026, Austin 3-1-1 phone service hours will be: Sunday–Saturday, 7 a.m.–9 p.m.  

After-hours options will still be available. Residents can submit non-emergency service requests outside of phone service hours by:

  • Using the Austin 3-1-1 mobile app
  • Submitting a request online at Austin311.org

        Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail - North Shore near Rainey St. and I-35 Bridge

        Date of detour: September 2025 to 2033
        Description: Due to the TxDOT I-35 Capital Express Central project, Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail users should expect significant north shore trail reroutes beginning September 2025 and continuing through 2033. The detour, located along the south side of East Avenue, is designed to balance safety and accessibility without crossing traffic. All work on the trail and parkland is within TxDOT's right-of-way.

        TxDOT crews are constructing a dock just west of I-35 over the next few weeks, working between 7 a.m. and 5 p.m. The dock will be approximately the size of a basketball court and used to transport construction materials and equipment needed for reconstruction of the Lady Bird Lake bridge. The community can expect an increase in noise, lighting and truck traffic in the area.

        TxDOT operations will happen on the north shore of Lady Bird Lake west of the boat ramp near the Holiday Inn between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. nightly to ensure that the trail is operational during daytime hours. The "Holiday Inn" boat ramp west of I-35 is reserved for emergency vehicles for the duration of the construction.

        Trail Reroute Construction Timeline

        • September 15, 2025: Parking lot under the I-35 bridge (north shore of Lady Bird Lake) closes.
        • Late September/Early October 2025: Detour west of I-35 opens (construction expected to take 3 weeks).
        • Late November/Early December 2025: Detour east of I-35 opens following construction in November.

        East Avenue between the southbound I-35 frontage road and Nash Hernandez Sr. Road (under the I-35 bridge) is closed as crews install utilities, through early 2026. 

        Beginning on Thursday, August 20, TxDOT crews will begin long-term utility work on the Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail west of the I-35 bridges. As a result, the trail will be rerouted back to the permanent Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail for approximately two months (September-October 2026). Later this year, TxDOT crews will have intermittent trail detours switching between the existing and detour paths for approximately three months (November 2026-January 2027). Please take care to slow down as you approach this area and follow the directions of onsite workers. 

        Updated: August 19, 2026

            Partial Closures

              Walter E. Long Metropolitan Park - Boat Ramps

              Date of closure: October 16, 2024 until further notice
              Description: The boat ramps in Walter E. Long Metropolitan Park at Lake Walter E. Long are closed until further notice as water levels are too low for boaters to safely enter and exit the lake. Lake access will remain available for shore-launched watercraft such as canoes and kayaks. All other park amenities, including volleyball courts, picnic sites, grills, and walking trails, will remain available. Austin Energy will be upgrading Decker Dam's nearly 60-year-old infrastructure. In preparation for upcoming equipment upgrades, the lake level will be gradually lowered through natural evaporation and the boat ramps will remain closed for the duration of the project for the safety of boaters. The boat ramps will reopen when water levels once again allow boaters to safely enter and exit. For more information on the repairs to Decker Dam, visit PublicInput.com/DeckerDam.
